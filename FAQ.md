# Frequently asked questions

## Who can give Autopilot work?

Only the people its owner allows. They hand it work by delegating a Linear issue to Autopilot.

## Does a person have to merge its pull requests?

No. Autopilot merges a pull request by itself once CI is green and its own AI review approves the change.

## How do I know a change really works?

After the merge, Autopilot opens the live site in a browser and checks the result before it closes the ticket.
